## Goal
Maya, Arnold and RenderMan are installed under educational or non-commercial
licenses, which do not cover a monetized channel. Pick how 3D gets made for
content that will earn money.

## Options
1. Blender for channel content; keep Maya for coursework and portfolio.
   Free. Recommended.
2. Maya Indie when revenue justifies it. Check it is sold in the user's
   country and what RenderMan commercial costs alongside it.
3. Keep 3D out of monetized videos for now and ship 2D only.
